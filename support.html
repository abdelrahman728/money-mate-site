Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap Continue as Guest to start right away. Your data is backed up anonymously. Create an account later from the account icon on the Dashboard to access your data on another device or after reinstalling.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
On your current iPhone, create an account (Sign in with Apple or email) from the account icon on the Dashboard. Then sign in with the same account on the new iPhone.

What happens to my data if I delete the app as a guest?
Guest data is tied to that installation. If you delete the app without creating an account first, you cannot get that data back.

How do I change my currency?
Open the Categories tab and pick your currency.

How do I delete my account?
Tap the account icon on the Dashboard, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of its data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the email we send you.
