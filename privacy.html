Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Money Mate (“the app”, “we”) is developed by Abdelrahman Ibrahim (Plain Apps). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect as little as possible.

The short version

  • We store only what you enter in the app and what is needed for your account.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

Information we handle

Financial information you enter. Expenses (amount, date, category and optional note), your spending categories, net worth entries (title, type and amount) and your preferred currency. We use this only to show it back to you and to sync it between your devices.

Account information.

  • Guest mode: the app creates an anonymous account identifier so your data can be backed up. No email or name is collected.
  • Email sign-in: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Sign in with Apple: an identifier provided by Apple and the email address you choose to share (which may be an Apple private relay address). We do not request your name.

Information we do not collect. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, bank credentials, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device and synced to our backend, which is hosted by Supabase, acting as our data processor. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to it is restricted so that each account can read and change only its own records. Our servers may be located outside your country.

The Money Mate home screen widget reads a summary (this month’s spending and your net worth) that is stored on your device only.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, except with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage and authentication, Apple for Sign in with Apple), or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete your account: in the app, tap the account icon on the Dashboard, then Delete Account. Your account and all of its expenses, categories and net worth entries are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Sign out: removes your data from the device; it stays in your account so you can sign back in.
  • Unused guest accounts that have not connected for an extended period may be deleted automatically.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing plainapps8@gmail.com.

Children

Money Mate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact
